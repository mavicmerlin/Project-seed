Connect Device

1. Power On Your Device

Make sure your FlavorStation-S3 is powered on and within range (10 meters). The LED should be blinking blue.

2. Enable Bluetooth

Ensure Bluetooth is enabled on your computer or mobile device. Chrome or Edge browsers are recommended for Web Bluetooth API support.

3. Click Connect

Select "FlavorStation-S3" from the browser popup that appears. You may need to grant permission for the site to access Bluetooth devices.
