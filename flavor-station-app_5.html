Connect Device

1. Power On Your Device

Ensure your FlavorStation-S3 is powered on and within Bluetooth range (approximately 10 meters).

2. Enable Bluetooth

Make sure Bluetooth is enabled on your computer or mobile device. The browser will request permission to scan.

3. Select Device

Click "Connect Now" and select "FlavorStation-S3" from the list of available devices in the popup window.
